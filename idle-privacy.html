2. Data processed by the game

The game does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking, and it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID). On iOS the player ID and sign-in token are additionally stored in your device's keychain, so your save game can be restored after reinstalling the app; this data stays on your device.

3. Google Firebase

Save games and link codes are stored with Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) in the data center Frankfurt am Main (europe-west3). Google acts as processor. A transfer to third countries cannot be completely ruled out for Google services; Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

4. Retention

Your save game is stored as long as the player ID is used and deleted no later than 24 months after the last use. Of the daily backup copies only the last 10 are kept. Link codes expire after 15 minutes and are deleted when used.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). To delete your data, send an email to ehmannjakob@gmail.com. So we can find your save game, please include a code from Settings → “Link device” (you don't need to redeem it). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

7. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical access data (e.g. IP address) to deliver the page and for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited), which also receives your IP address. The website does not use cookies or tracking.

8. Changes

We will update this privacy policy when the game or the law changes, in particular before features such as optional ads or purchases are added. The current version is always available on this page.
