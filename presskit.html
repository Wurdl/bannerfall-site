About the game

Bannerfall is a cute but tactical lane battle game: build your army, rain fire on the enemy and storm their castle before your own falls.

Every battle plays differently: each level-up offers three cards – new units, upgrades, abilities or spells. Between battles, players collect and ascend 22 units, forge gear with elemental effects, pick talent paths and take on a raid with eight bosses.

The game is fair to play: the whole campaign is free, there is no energy system, and videos are only shown when the player chooses one.

Key features

  • 22 units, each transforming twice when ascended
  • 3 spells and a hero that can turn a battle
  • A new card choice at every level-up
  • Forge with fire, ice, poison, lightning and light
  • Talent paths for every class
  • Raid with 8 bosses and a visible set armour
  • 6 worlds with boss stages
  • Tournaments, leaderboard, friends and invite codes

About the developer

Bannerfall is made by Jakob Ehmann, a solo developer from Styria, Austria. Development started at the end of September 2026; about six weeks later the game launched on the App Store, with a public beta on iPhone and Android in between.
