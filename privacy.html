3. Google Firebase

Save games, leaderboard entries and game events are stored with Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) in the data center Frankfurt am Main (europe-west3). Google acts as processor. A transfer to third countries cannot be completely ruled out for Google services; Google relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional reward videos (e.g. to double the reward after a battle). These are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). When an ad is requested or shown, Google processes data such as your IP address, device and app information, the app-specific vendor identifier and your interactions with the ad, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown (consent dialog by Google). Without consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → “Privacy settings” in the game. If you buy “Ad-free”, no ad videos are shown. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed exclusively by Apple (App Store). We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation of which product was bought, to credit it in the game.

6. Retention

Save game, leaderboard entry and game events are stored as long as the player ID is used and deleted no later than 24 months after the last use. Transfer codes expire after 24 hours.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. To delete your data, an email with your player ID (shown in the game's settings) to ehmannjakob@gmail.com is enough. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. In the EU, personalized advertising requires the consent of a parent or guardian for users under 16; younger players should not consent to personalized ads.

9. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technical access data (e.g. IP address) to deliver the page and for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited), which also receives your IP address. The website does not use cookies or tracking.

10. Changes

We will update this privacy policy when the game or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
