Support

Questions, feedback, a bug or a purchase problem? Write to ehmannjakob@gmail.com. Please include your player ID (Settings in the game, shown at the bottom) and your device model.

Frequently asked questions

I bought something but didn't receive it.
Open Settings in the game and tap “Restore purchases”. If that doesn't help, email us with your player ID and the date of purchase.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
On the old phone: Settings → “Transfer progress” → create a code. Enter the code on the new phone within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?
Email us with your player ID and we'll delete your save, leaderboard entry and statistics. See also the privacy policy.

Can I join the beta?
Yes! Send us a short email and we'll invite you to TestFlight.
